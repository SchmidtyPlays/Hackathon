Key Risks of Malicious Links

Ransomware:

A single click can encrypt your files and demand a payment for their release.

Malware Infections:

Some links can trigger the hidden downloads of viruses, spyware, or trojans that can steal data, monitor activity, or give attackers remote access to your device.

Network & Data Breaches:

Malicious links can compromise your network security and lead to unauthorized access to sensitive data.

Financial Loss:

Malicious links can result in direct financial harm through fraud, identity theft, or other scams.
